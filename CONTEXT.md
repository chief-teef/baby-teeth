# Baby Teeth

A scrapbook blog whose authored entries can be read publicly after publication.

## Language

**Entry**:
An authored scrapbook containing one or more ordered pages published together under one stable identity, whether draft, public, or trashed.
_Avoid_: Post when a distinction from an announcement matters.

**Page**:
A single paper face within an entry. An entry's pages stay together in the book.
_Avoid_: Entry when referring to one face of a multi-page entry.

**Public entry**:
An entry currently available to readers at its public permalink.
_Avoid_: Saved entry, publish click.

**Announcement**:
A Discord message inviting readers to a public entry.
_Avoid_: Publication, entry.

**Confirmed announcement**:
An announcement whose delivery was acknowledged and recorded.
_Avoid_: Exactly-once delivery.

**Unconfirmed announcement**:
An announcement attempt whose delivery has not been acknowledged and recorded; a message may already exist.
_Avoid_: Unsent message.

**Announcement baseline**:
The set of public entries excluded from announcements when the feature is first activated.
_Avoid_: Sent history.
